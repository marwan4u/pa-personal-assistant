PA Personal Assistant — Data Deletion Instructions

Updated: October 1, 2026

PA is a private project for two authorized users. To request deletion of data held by PA, contact the project owner using the contact email shown in the Meta app listing, with the subject “PA Data Deletion Request”. Specify which PA data or connected account should be deleted; do not include passwords or access tokens.

The owner will verify the request with the authorized user, review what information PA actually holds, delete applicable PA-controlled records and files, and confirm completion or explain any retention limits. The current WhatsApp test webhook does not intentionally save incoming message content to PA's database. Service-provider logs or information held independently by Meta, Google or other services may need separate deletion through those providers.

For information about planned processing, read the privacy policy.
