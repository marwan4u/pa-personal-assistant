PA Personal Assistant — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

PA is a private personal-assistant project initially intended for its owner and the owner's wife, with other individually authorized family members potentially added later. It is not offered as a public service. This policy describes the intended service and the limited WhatsApp test integration currently operating. Features described as planned are not yet active.

Information handled

During testing, Meta may deliver WhatsApp messages, sender identifiers, message metadata and delivery events to PA's webhook. The current test webhook verifies the request and may send a fixed test reply to an authorized test recipient. It does not currently save incoming WhatsApp messages or use them for AI processing. Operational logs may contain request status and limited diagnostic information.

Planned, separately enabled features may process messages and documents deliberately sent to PA, as well as information users explicitly authorize PA to access through connected services. PA is not designed to read unrelated personal WhatsApp conversations.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to operate, secure and test the private assistant and, once enabled, provide requested assistant functions. Technical service providers used or planned include Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, Vercel, Supabase, Google services and an AI service provider. Each provider processes information according to the services actually enabled and its applicable terms. PA is not intended to sell personal data or provide it to advertisers.

Access, retention and security

Access is restricted to individually authorized users. The planned architecture separates each user's private emails, documents and reminders from explicitly shared family information. Sharing PA's WhatsApp business number does not grant access to another user's private information. Each connected Gmail account requires authorization from its account holder. The integration uses server-side credentials and webhook signature verification. Test webhook message content is not intentionally persisted in the PA database at this stage; platform and provider logs may be retained under their own policies. Before document storage or AI processing is activated, access controls, retention and deletion procedures will be reviewed and this policy updated.

Choices and deletion

Users can stop sending information to PA and request deletion of any PA-held information. See the data deletion instructions. Information independently held by Meta or other connected services may require separate action with those providers.

Contact

As PA is a private project, its owner manages privacy and deletion requests through the contact email published in the Meta app listing.
